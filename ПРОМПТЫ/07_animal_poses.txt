===== PROMPT =====
ONE ATTACHED IMAGE: the animals sheet of this game. Draw THE SAME 9 animals, keeping each one's look, colors, size and painting style exactly. Every pose must have the same scale as that animal's full-body idle drawing in the attached image.

THE 9 ANIMALS (columns, left to right): 1 rabid dog, 2 giant toad, 3 raptor, 4 giant scorpion, 5 armored lizard, 6 snow wolf, 7 deer, 8 boar, 9 hare.

VIEW — critical: every animal faces RIGHT, seen from the side and slightly from above, like the attached image.

ART STYLE: painted semi-realistic, slightly chunky proportions, mutated post-apocalyptic look, light from the top-left.

BACKGROUND AND EDGES — critical: perfectly flat solid magenta #FF00FF (if the animal itself is pink or purple, use solid blue #0000FF for the whole sheet). No ground, no shadow, no snow or dust under the feet. Hard, clean, fully opaque edges.

FORBIDDEN: blood, gore, smoke, glow halos, text, letters, numbers, logos, watermark, grid lines, labels.

SHEET FORMAT: one image, invisible grid of 9 columns and 3 rows of equal cells, one drawing per cell, centered, at least 12% empty margin, drawings never touch. Column = animal (order above). Row = pose:
Row 1 — RUN A: legs fully stretched, front legs forward, back legs back (toad and hare: mid-jump, body stretched; scorpion: legs spread, tail up)
Row 2 — RUN B: legs gathered under the body (toad and hare: crouched before a jump)
Row 3 — ATTACK: dog, wolf, raptor — biting lunge with open jaws; toad — mouth wide open, tongue just starting to come out; scorpion — tail stinger striking forward over its head; armored lizard — head lowered, charging; boar — tusks forward, charging; deer — rearing up on hind legs; hare — ears up, alert, about to flee
