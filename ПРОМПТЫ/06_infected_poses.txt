ONE ATTACHED IMAGE: the infected enemies sheet of this game. Draw THE SAME 7 characters, keeping each one's face, clothes, colors, body shape, size and painting style exactly. Every pose of a character must have the same height and scale as that character's full-body idle drawing in the attached image.

THE 7 CHARACTERS (columns, left to right): 1 walker (ragged shirt, slow shambling zombie), 2 crawler (legless zombie dragging itself on its arms), 3 spitter (swamp zombie with a swollen throat sac), 4 sprinter (thin fast zombie), 5 bloated (huge fat zombie covered in blisters), 6 soldier (zombie in a helmet and torn military gear), 7 glowing (irradiated zombie in a long coat, skin painted with sickly green light — painted only, no glow halo).

VIEW — critical: every character faces RIGHT, seen from the side and slightly from above, like the attached image.

ART STYLE: painted semi-realistic, slightly chunky proportions, creepy but not gory, light from the top-left.

BACKGROUND AND EDGES — critical: perfectly flat solid magenta #FF00FF. No ground, no shadow, no puddles. Hard, clean, fully opaque edges.

FORBIDDEN: blood splashes, gore, smoke, glow halos, text, letters, numbers, logos, watermark, grid lines, labels.

SHEET FORMAT: one image, invisible grid of 7 columns and 4 rows of equal cells, one drawing per cell, centered, at least 12% empty margin, drawings never touch. Column = character (order above). Row = pose:
Row 1 — WALK A: front leg forward, back leg behind, arms swinging (crawler: right arm reaching far forward, body low)
Row 2 — WALK B: the opposite step, legs passing each other (crawler: both arms pulling, body slid forward)
Row 3 — ATTACK: lunging forward with arms or mouth (spitter: head thrown forward spitting, mouth wide; bloated: belly pushed forward, arms raised; soldier: swinging a rifle butt)
Row 4 — HURT: staggering backward, head thrown back, arms flailing
